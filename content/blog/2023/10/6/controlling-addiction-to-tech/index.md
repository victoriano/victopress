I believe we've all accepted that our phones are like sugar and distract us more than we would want. iOS’s Screen Time is garbage; it's really too easy to bypass the limits you set for yourself.

Almost two years ago, I installed the [@one_sec_app](https://twitter.com/one_sec_app) on my iPhone, making me wait 20 seconds every time I try to open Twitter or Instagram. They allow you to export data of all the times you end up opening them and I analyze it with [@graphext](https://twitter.com/graphext). Here you can clearly see how I went from opening social media around 10 times a day to 2-3. The pattern is clear; I use social networks more when I am alone and traveling.

This demonstrates the lack of effort companies like [@Apple](https://twitter.com/Apple) or [@Google](https://twitter.com/Google) put into creating operating systems that truly help us have a better relationship with our phones.
