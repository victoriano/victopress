*15K thousand people participated in *[*a Twitter meme*](https://twitter.com/search?q=%22First+language%22+AND+%22difficulties%22&src=typed_query&f=live)* where they were asked to express their relationship with programming languages.*

*We collected and analyzed with *[*Graphext*](http://www.graphext.com/)* all the answers. Here are the main insights that I found.*

![](/api/images/blog/2019/10/12/analyzing-the-complex-relationship-between-the-most-loved-hated-and-used-programming-languages/image-01.jpg "text-width")

Each node is a person and their tweet, those who **answered more similarly get connected **([Graphext](http://www.graphext.com/) dimensionality reduction algorithm calculate a similarity score) In the extremes you find people that mentioned the same language for all the categories), like

[Ryan Florence](https://medium.com/u/162352c45b6e?source=post_page-----4f7ae105e843--------------------------------)

The question that correlates more with the rest of is about what is the programming language is more used. In the image we can see a clear map of **web developers on the right** (Javascript, PHP, Ruby) and **data scientists** on the **left** (R, Python) and **C/C++, Java in the middle**.

![](/api/images/blog/2019/10/12/analyzing-the-complex-relationship-between-the-most-loved-hated-and-used-programming-languages/image-02.jpg "text-width")

Among the most **loved** programming languages, **Python wins over the most used, Javascript**. In the top 10 we can find 2 less used languages but emerging with many fans already: **Go** and **Rust.**

![](/api/images/blog/2019/10/12/analyzing-the-complex-relationship-between-the-most-loved-hated-and-used-programming-languages/image-03.jpg "text-width")

When we see the ranking of the **most hated programming languages** and their distribution, it’s clear that** almost everybody hates Java** no matter what other languages they use the most. **Javascript** is mostly hated among non web developers but also by people that use it all the time.

![](/api/images/blog/2019/10/12/analyzing-the-complex-relationship-between-the-most-loved-hated-and-used-programming-languages/image-04.jpg "text-width")

**Python** wins by far when it comes to recommending a language **for beginners **for almost most programmers, except for web developers that go for Javascript. **Ruby** is quite recommended relatively respect to its usage.

![](/api/images/blog/2019/10/12/analyzing-the-complex-relationship-between-the-most-loved-hated-and-used-programming-languages/image-05.jpg "text-width")

**Basic, Pascal, QBASIC, Visual Basic**… stand out as programming languages that were **learnt first** by the people who participated in the crowdsourced survey meme. But **none of these languages are recommended today** to learn programming.

![](/api/images/blog/2019/10/12/analyzing-the-complex-relationship-between-the-most-loved-hated-and-used-programming-languages/image-06.jpg "text-width")

The programming languages that** give more headaches** are **C** and **C ++** and they go for Python. Many who use Javascript have some **trauma with Java**. There are many those who freak out with **functional programming** (**Haskell**, Lisp) or with declarative (**Prolog**) or **Assembly** in college.
