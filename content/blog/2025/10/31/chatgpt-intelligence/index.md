There are still many people who tend to underestimate GPT-5 and other models because they judge their value based on quick questions, not on what they could actually achieve with well-thought-out prompts and workflows. Having access to a superior mind doesn’t automatically make you better in that field. Just like the people close to Einstein didn’t become physics experts simply by being around him, or his PhD students didn’t surpass him, we won’t make the most out of GPT-5 without a lot of intention, judgment, and method. And many people can’t even see that.
